Free Print lets you print and scan with a USB printer plugged straight into your phone with an OTG adapter. It needs no printer driver, no print server, no Wi-Fi and no internet connection. The app has no internet permission at all, collects nothing and shows no ads.

<b>Print</b>
• PDFs and pictures, several at once and mixed in one job
• Live preview of every sheet, a thumbnail strip, swipe between sheets and a zoomable full-screen view
• Page ranges ("1-3, 5, 8-10"), odd or even pages, tap-to-pick page numbers
• 1 to 8 pages per sheet, fit / fill / actual size, scaling, margins, position, orientation
• A4, Letter, A5, A6, B5, Legal, Executive, 4 x 6, 5 x 7 and 8 x 10 paper
• Collated copies, draft / normal / high quality, colour or grayscale
• Manual two-sided printing for printers without a duplexer, with an animated guide for turning the stack
• Pause a job between sheets
• "Optimize for print" for photographed documents: whitens the paper, evens out shadows, deepens the ink, page by page and undoable

<b>Scan</b>
• Scan from the printer's glass over the same USB cable (eSCL / AirScan)
• Scan several pages, delete and restore pages, optimize them
• Save as a PDF or as pictures, as scanned or laid out on paper sheets with the print settings
• Share the result, or print a copy

<b>Works with the rest of your phone</b>
• Share PDFs, pictures and text to Free Print from any app, or open files with it
• A system print service: print from any app through Android's own print dialog
• English and Sinhala, with a language switch inside the app

<b>Requirements</b>
A phone with USB host (OTG) support running Android 8 or newer on a 64-bit ARM processor (arm64-v8a; phones that run 32-bit ARM only cannot install it), an OTG adapter, and a printer that exposes the standard IPP-over-USB interface (most printers sold in the last decade), or a Samsung ML-2160 (black and white only). Tested with a Canon PIXMA G2020 on several phones; other printers are expected to work but have not all been tried.
