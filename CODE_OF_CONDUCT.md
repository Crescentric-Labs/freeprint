# Code of conduct

Free Print is a small project run by volunteers. We want it to be a friendly, useful place for anyone who prints, scans, files
a report or sends a patch.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to Android, printers or open source.
- Assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Keep reports and reviews specific and constructive: what happened, what you expected, what could change.
- Respect other people's time and privacy. Do not post anyone's personal details, and remove personal data (names, addresses,
  document contents) from logs and screenshots before sharing them.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language or jokes.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or repeatedly pushing a point after a decision has been made.
- Publishing private information about others without their consent.
- Spam, advertising, or unrelated promotion in issues, discussions or pull requests.

## Scope

This applies to the repository, its issues, discussions and pull requests, and to anyone representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people who break these rules. To report a problem,
use [GitHub's private report](https://github.com/Crescentric-Labs/freeprint/security/advisories/new) and say it is a conduct
matter, or contact a maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled privately and in good faith.

This text is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
